# Entrepreneurial WIL Projects (14 July - 30 October)

## Repository Overview

This portfolio documents the evidence and STAR reflections from the Entrepreneurial Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) project completed between 14 July and 30 October.

During the project, we were tasked with developing a business concept and breaking the idea into manageable micro-elements. We organised the work into sprints and presented our progress from Tuesday to Friday, covering solution architecture, Product Backlogs, user stories and proposed business solutions.

Our team developed **D6 Food Bulk**, a business concept designed to make food purchasing more convenient and financially manageable for students. The platform connects NSFAS students with wholesalers offering affordable student rates and allows students to create customised food bulks based on their needs and budgets. The initial service area focuses on CPUT residences at the Cape Town Campus.

## Project Link

- **Live Website:** [D6 Food Bulk](https://cptdfb.vercel.app/)

## Agile Planning Summary

The following summary explains how the D6 Food Bulk concept was translated into user-focused requirements and manageable Agile work.

### User Stories and Tasks

The user stories describe the value that each type of user should receive from the platform.

- **Affordable product discovery:** As an NSFAS student, I want to view food products at student rates so that I can identify affordable options.
- **Customised food bulks:** As a student, I want to select products for my own food bulk so that it suits my needs and available budget.
- **Cost visibility:** As a student, I want to see the total cost of my selected products so that I can make an informed financial decision.
- **Wholesaler visibility:** As a wholesaler, I want students to see my available products and rates so that I can serve the student market.

Supporting tasks included clarifying acceptance criteria, designing the student journey, developing product and customisation features, validating prices, and testing the completed behaviour.

### Product Backlog

The Product Backlog contains all known work for D6 Food Bulk, ordered according to student value, urgency, dependencies, and available capacity.

- **Core priorities:** Student-rate product listings, food-bulk customisation, price calculation, and order information.
- **Supporting priorities:** Product availability, responsive design, validation, testing, and usability improvements.
- **Future priorities:** Additional wholesaler tools and expansion beyond the initial CPUT Cape Town Campus residences.

The backlog was refined as the team received feedback and gained a better understanding of the business concept.

### Sprint Backlog

The Sprint Backlog contained Product Backlog items selected for short delivery periods. It gave the team a clear focus for development and the Tuesday-to-Friday presentations.

- Refine the business problem, target users, and proposed solution.
- Prepare and present the solution architecture.
- Write user stories, acceptance criteria, and supporting tasks.
- Prioritise Product Backlog items and select achievable sprint work.
- Develop and test product browsing, customisation, and pricing features.
- Demonstrate progress, record feedback, and refine the next sprint.
