# User Stories and Tasks Evidence

The D6 Food Bulk user stories were refined as the team learned more about student affordability, convenience and safety.

## User Story 1 — Affordable and Customisable Orders

**Story:** As a student, I want affordable grocery bundles that I can customise according to my needs and budget.

**Work recorded:** The team identified bulk bundles, validated grocery and supplier prices, and defined student discounts and the Bulk Wallet concept.

## User Story 2 — Simple Ordering and Payment

**Story:** As a student, I want to place and pay for an order easily.

**Work recorded:** The ordering and payment steps were refined, and the Place and Pay process was mapped.

## User Story 3 — Same-Day Residence Delivery

**Story:** As a student, I want same-day delivery to my residence.

**Work recorded:** The team investigated delivery costs and partners and proposed a R45 fee with a same-day delivery window.

## User Story 4 — Safe Collection and Refunds

**Story:** As a student, I want a safe and traceable handover and the ability to request a refund when items are not in good condition.

**Work recorded:** Block Agent responsibilities, verified orders and safe handovers were defined, while the refund process required further clarification.
