# Product Backlog Evidence

The Product Backlog is the ordered list of work needed to develop and improve D6 Food Bulk. The team considered student value, urgency, dependencies, effort, and available capacity when reviewing priorities.

## Core Priorities

- Display available food products and student rates.
- Allow students to create customised food bulks.
- Calculate and display the total cost of selected products.
- Capture the information required to process an order.

## Supporting Priorities

- Keep wholesaler product availability accurate.
- Validate student and order information.
- Improve responsive behaviour and usability.
- Test completed features and resolve defects.

## Future Priorities

- Add further wholesaler-management capabilities.
- Expand the platform beyond the initial CPUT Cape Town Campus residences.
- Refine future features in response to student and stakeholder feedback.

The backlog changed as the team learned more about the business concept and reviewed feedback during presentations.
