# User Stories and Tasks Evidence

User stories helped the team describe D6 Food Bulk requirements from the user's perspective. Each story was divided into smaller design, development, validation, and testing tasks.

## User Story Examples

### Affordable Product Discovery

**Story:** As an NSFAS student, I want to view food products at student rates so that I can identify affordable options.

**Tasks:** Confirm product information, display prices and availability, and test the product listing on different screen sizes.

### Customised Food Bulks

**Story:** As a student, I want to select products for my own food bulk so that it suits my needs and available budget.

**Tasks:** Define the customisation rules, build the product-selection experience, update the selected bulk, and test valid selections.

### Cost Visibility

**Story:** As a student, I want to see the total cost of my selected products so that I can make an informed financial decision.

**Tasks:** Implement the price calculation, update the total when products change, and verify calculation accuracy.

### Wholesaler Visibility

**Story:** As a wholesaler, I want students to see my available products and rates so that I can serve the student market.

**Tasks:** Structure wholesaler product information, show student pricing and availability, and test unavailable-product behaviour.
