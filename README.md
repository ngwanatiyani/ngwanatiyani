# Entrepreneurial WIL Projects (14 July - 30 October)

## Repository Overview

This portfolio documents the evidence and STAR reflections from the Entrepreneurial Work-Integrated Learning (WIL) project completed between 14 July and 30 October.

During the project, we were tasked with developing a business concept and breaking the idea into manageable micro-elements. We organised the work into sprints and presented our progress from Tuesday to Friday, covering solution architecture, Product Backlogs, user stories and proposed business solutions.

Our team developed **D6 Food Bulk**, a business concept designed to make food purchasing more convenient and financially manageable for students. The platform connects NSFAS students with wholesalers offering affordable student rates and allows students to create customised food bulks based on their needs and budgets. The initial service area focuses on CPUT residences at the Cape Town Campus.

## Project Link

- **Live Website:** [D6 Food Bulk](https://cptdfb.vercel.app/)

## Agile Planning Summary

The following summary explains the user stories and backlog work recorded in the D6 Food Bulk taskboards and Agile Logbooks.

### User Stories and Tasks

The stories were refined during the sprints as the team gained a clearer understanding of student needs.

- **Affordable and customisable orders:** As a student, I want affordable grocery bundles that I can customise according to my needs and budget.
- **Simple ordering and payment:** As a student, I want to place and pay for an order easily.
- **Same-day delivery:** As a student, I want same-day delivery to my residence.
- **Safe collection and refunds:** As a student, I want a safe, traceable handover and the ability to request a refund when items are not in good condition.

Supporting tasks included validating supplier prices, defining student discounts and the Bulk Wallet, mapping the Place and Pay process, confirming delivery costs and partners, and clarifying Block Agent responsibilities.

### Product Backlog

The Product Backlog captured work that still required validation or refinement.

- Validate grocery prices, supplier prices and student demand.
- Refine the ordering and payment steps.
- Confirm delivery partners, the proposed R45 delivery fee and the same-day delivery window.
- Define Block Agent responsibilities, safe handovers and the refund process.

Completed outcomes included the student discount and Bulk Wallet concepts, the Place and Pay process map, a proposed same-day delivery model, and verified order handovers.

### Sprint Backlog

The Sprint Backlog organised selected work into short reporting periods and supported the Tuesday-to-Friday presentations.

- **21–24 July:** Reviewed the problem and target market, presented the business idea, mapped Place and Pay, Pool and Fulfil, and Deliver and Collect, and prepared the presentation.
- **28–31 July:** Updated Trello user stories and sprint logs, refined the value proposition, presented the concept and improved the ordering and delivery process.
- **4–7 August:** Reviewed the business model, presented the concept, started coding the HTML frontend, researched hosting platforms and prepared the product for deployment.

The main blockers were limited sprint time and unconfirmed supplier and delivery costs. For the complete cards, assignments and progress history, visit the project board.
